F1 Dynasty beta privacy policy

13 October 2026

F1 Dynasty's beta is a single-player racing team-management game. No game account sign-in is required. Cloud synchronization is disabled for this beta.

Your careers, settings and progress are stored on your device or in the browser storage for the site you use. The beta website at beta.f1dynasty.com has its own browser origin. It cannot automatically read localStorage, IndexedDB, service workers or cache storage belonging to f1dynasty.com. Careers are not automatically transferred between the production website, beta website and mobile applications.

You can intentionally export a JSON save backup and import it on another supported installation. Backups contain your game state, including your chosen manager/team names and career history. Keep backups private if you do not want to disclose that information. Export invokes the device's file/share interface or a browser download; the game does not automatically send the file to us. Import validates the selected file through the existing game persistence boundary. Native imports require review and an empty destination slot; full storage does not silently replace another career.

The prepared web beta blocks external authentication, cloud-save and analytics network requests using its beta-only Content Security Policy and disabled cloud client. It does not initialize a production cloud account or synchronize a career into production cloud records. Parent-domain cookies can technically be sent to subdomains by browsers; the beta game does not read cookies or use them to sign into cloud services. This is not a claim that all cookies are isolated by origin.

Hosting services necessarily handle requests to deliver the website and downloads, which may include IP addresses, requested URLs and browser/network information under their own policies. The prepared website and APK download are hosted through GitHub after owner activation; Cloudflare provides the domain's DNS. This draft does not promise an unverified provider retention period or absence of provider logs.

Bug reports are voluntary. The in-game feedback action prepares a template containing platform, version, screen and game season/round context. You choose whether to send it and what to attach. Do not include passwords, account tokens or private save files in public reports. Email feedback to f1dynastygame@gmail.com, use Apple's TestFlight feedback, or post to the existing F1 Dynasty Reddit community. Email and Reddit reports are processed by those services under their policies; Reddit posts may be public.

Apple's TestFlight distribution and feedback/crash systems have their own data handling. See Apple TestFlight and Apple privacy information. The fact that Apple receives beta diagnostics does not mean the game uploads your local career to a cloud-save account.

To remove locally stored game data, use the application's save-management controls or the browser/device's storage controls. Export any career you wish to keep first. Removing local storage does not remove copies of backups or reports that you intentionally sent elsewhere. Contact f1dynastygame@gmail.com about a report you sent; provide enough information to identify it without sending passwords or tokens.

This policy must be reviewed again before enabling cloud synchronization, adding automatic telemetry or changing data handling. No such change is part of the current release preparation.
